Flatten SF

Find the flattest route between any two points in San Francisco · October 2026

Pick a start and an end, then slide from the shortest route to the flattest one. Every route on the slider is one that nothing else beats on both distance and climbing. Routing runs entirely in your browser across 160,000 street segments, with elevation from USGS 1-meter lidar.

The analysis behind it maps the city's hidden network of flat streets. The Wiggle falls out of the algorithm on its own. The Mission valley floor and the Panhandle chain turn out to be the two spines that low-gradient traffic is forced onto, and a handful of passes over Golden Gate Park and around Twin Peaks decide most east–west trips.

Learning Jazz Pianist Style with Cross-Attention Conditioning

ISMIR 2026, Abu Dhabi · with Akira Maezawa and Simon Dixon

Can a model learn not just to recognize who is playing, but to play in their manner? We fine-tune Aria, a transformer pretrained on piano MIDI, on solo recordings of twelve jazz pianists, adding gated cross-attention over learned pianist embeddings. Conditioned continuations are attributed to the intended pianist 70% of the time, against 37% without conditioning, and a classifier trained only on the model's own generations identifies real pianists with 95% song-level accuracy.

The demo page continues one prompt in twelve styles. Sound on.
